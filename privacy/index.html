Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

This policy explains how the Mega7Products website handles information and the choices available to visitors. Contact: mega7products2@gmail.com.

Information handled by this website

The site has no account system, purchase checkout, or contact form. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, for security and operation of its service under its own privacy statement. Mega7Products does not operate an application database for visitor accounts or purchases.

Advertising measurement

The existing Google Ads tag (AW-17961565555) is loaded only after you select “Allow ads measurement.” It can send visit and outgoing-click information to Google and may use cookies or other identifiers. No Google Analytics 4 tag is installed.

Your choice is stored locally in your browser as mega7-ads-choice-v1. You can reopen Advertising privacy choices in the footer. Declining future measurement reloads the page to stop the loaded tag; it does not erase information already sent or cookies already set by third parties.

Affiliate links and external websites

Following a product link takes you to the seller through an affiliate URL. That website and its partners may process referral information, set cookies, and handle purchases under their own terms. We do not receive your payment details through this site.

Your choices and requests

You may browse without enabling advertising measurement and manage cookies in your browser. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location and the operator’s activities.

For privacy questions or to exercise rights available under applicable law, email Mega7Products at mega7products2@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request.” Depending on applicable law, these rights may include access, correction, deletion, or objection to certain processing. We may need information reasonably necessary to verify a request; do not send identification documents unless an appropriate process is agreed.

Email correspondence and retention

If you email us, your email provider and ours process that correspondence. We use the contact details and information you choose to send to respond to your request and maintain relevant correspondence. We retain correspondence only as needed for that purpose, related recordkeeping, and applicable obligations. You can ask us about or request deletion of correspondence using the contact above. We do not ask you to send passwords, payment details, or sensitive health information.

Third-party services and international processing

GitHub, Google, the affiliate destination, and email providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their processing and retention are described in their respective policies. We do not promise a retention period for information held by those providers. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Google’s privacy policy. Review the seller’s privacy terms before following an affiliate link or completing a purchase.
